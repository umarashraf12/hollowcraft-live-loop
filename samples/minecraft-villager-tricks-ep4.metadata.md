## Description
3 Minecraft villager tricks that make you rich: cure a zombie villager for huge discounts, get the Mending book every time, and the easiest emeralds in the game. 💚📕

Trick #1, zombie villager cure: trap it out of the sun, hit it with a splash potion of weakness, then feed it a golden apple. After a few minutes it is cured, and its prices drop a lot (some to 1 emerald). On Hard difficulty, zombies always turn villagers into zombie villagers.
Trick #2, Mending every time: give a jobless villager a lectern. No Mending? Break the lectern and place it again to reroll its trades. Trade once to lock them in.
Trick #3, easiest emeralds: fletchers buy 32 sticks for 1 emerald. 4 logs = 32 sticks = 1 emerald.
