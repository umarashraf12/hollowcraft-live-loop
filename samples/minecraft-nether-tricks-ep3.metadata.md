## Description
3 Nether tricks that get you netherite faster: build a Nether portal without a diamond pickaxe, find ancient debris with beds at Y 15, and build a blue-ice boat highway that is faster than flying. 🔥

Trick #1, no-diamond portal: place lava where each frame block goes, then pour water on it to turn it into obsidian. A basic frame needs only 10 obsidian (corners are optional). Use a dirt mold to hold the lava in place.
Trick #2, ancient debris: go to Y 15, dig a tunnel, and use beds as explosives (beds explode in the Nether). Ancient debris is blast proof, so it is the only thing left. 4 netherite scrap + 4 gold ingots = 1 netherite ingot.
Trick #3, Nether ice highway: 1 block in the Nether = 8 in the Overworld. Blue ice + a boat goes over 70 blocks per second.
