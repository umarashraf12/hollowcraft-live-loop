# HollowCraft Shorts pilot: "Hollow, Pip & VOID"

Original cast (no third-party characters):
- **Hollow**: teal hoodie, calm and smart. Same voice as the main channel's narrator.
- **Pip**: Hollow's little brother. Orange hoodie, overconfident, higher voice.
- **VOID**: shadow villain with glowing purple eyes. Series rule: **VOID can't stand light.**

Formats are adapted from what the Maizen / SUDNAP / Ligabka analysis showed working: POOR vs RICH, security-house battles, and darkness-suspense
hooks ("…then X appeared"). Each episode ends on "follow for part N" to chain viewers into the next one.
Upload in order, about 1 per day, and pin each one to the next in the related-video link.
