## Settings (all three)
- **Category:** Gaming, with Minecraft as the game title
- **Language:** English. Captions are burned into the video, so no SRT is needed.
- **Altered/synthetic content:** No. These are stylised animation with stock TTS voices.
- **Audience:** these are cartoon stories that children will watch, and YouTube decides based on content, not your intent. Choosing "made for kids" turns off comments and some features. Recommended: post them on a **separate Shorts channel**, so the tricks channel stays "not made for kids".
- **Playlist:** "Hollow, Pip & VOID" (in order). Playlists help Shorts viewers binge.
- **Posting time:** pick one consistent time per day. After 48 hours, compare viewed vs swiped-away and change the hook text of the weakest one.
